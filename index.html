Subject Standard: AS 2 (Inventories) 100% ICAI Standard
Core Principle 1.5D LDR

Valuation Rule (AS 2 Para 5)

Inventories are valued at lower of cost and net realizable value (NRV). Cost includes purchase price, direct conversion costs, and expenses incurred to bring stock to its current location.

ICAI Marking Keyword: Item-by-item comparison of Cost vs NRV.
ICAI Past Exam May 2023 • 5 Marks

Raw Material X purchased at ₹150/unit. Current replacement cost is ₹120. If finished good sells at ₹220 (Total cost ₹200), calculate raw material valuation.

View ICAI Suggested Answer & Provision

Provision: Materials are NOT written down below cost if the finished products in which they are incorporated are expected to be sold at or above cost.

Conclusion: Valued at original cost ₹150 per unit.
