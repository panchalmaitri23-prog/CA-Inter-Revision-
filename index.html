Measurement Principle (Para 5)

Inventories must be valued at the lower of cost and net realizable value (NRV). Cost of inventories includes purchase cost, conversion cost, and other costs incurred in bringing items to present condition.

ICAI Mandatory Keyword: Comparison of Cost & NRV item-by-item basis.
ICAI Exam Question May 2023 • 5 Marks

Raw material X cost ₹150 per unit. Replacement cost is ₹120. If finished product sells at ₹220 (Cost ₹200), determine the valuation of raw material.

View ICAI Suggested Answer & Working Notes

Provision: Materials held for use in production are not written down below cost if the finished goods are expected to be sold at or above cost.

Conclusion: Valued at Cost ₹150 (not written down to ₹120 replacement cost).
