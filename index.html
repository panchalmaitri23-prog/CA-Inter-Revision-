Valuation Principle (AS 2 Para 5)

Inventories are valued at the lower of cost and net realizable value (NRV). Cost includes purchase price, direct conversion costs, and expenses incurred to bring stock to its current location.

ICAI Marking Keyword: Item-by-item comparison of Cost vs NRV.
